GPA

How does the 4.0 unweighted GPA work?

The type of class isn't considered in this GPA. The weights of each grade is as follows:

Letter Grade Numerical Grade Weight
A 90-100 4.0
B 80-89 3.0
C 70-79 2.0
D 60-69 1.0
F <60 0.0

How does the 4.0 weighted GPA work?

The type of class is considered in this GPA. The weights of each grade in consideration of class is as follows:

Letter Grade Numerical Grade Academic Pre-AP AP
A 90-100 4.0 4.5 5.0
B 80-89 3.0 3.5 4.0
C 70-79 2.0 2.5 3.0
D 60-69 1.0 1.5 2.0
F <60 0.0 0.0 0.0

How does the 100-point based GPA work?

The type of class is considered in this GPA, but in a different scale. No matter what grade you get in the class, your numerical grade is multiplied by the weight of the type of class and added to a total and averaged out by the total credits you have. The weights of each class are as follows:

Type Weight
AP/Dual/OnRamps 1.3
Pre-AP 1.2
Academic 1.1

Grading Period

How does 6-weeks average work?

The average major grades and minor grades of a 6-weeks grading period is combined. The weights of major grades and minor grades differ depending on the type of class. The weights are as follows:

Type Academic Pre-AP or AP
Major 60% 70%
Minor 40% 30%

How does semester average work?

Every 6-Weeks average is combined and averaged. One 6-Weeks is worth 26.66%, and the other two are worth 26.67% of the average. If the final exam was taken, it accounts for 20% of the semester average.

What is the square root curve?

The final grade is square rooted and multiplied by 10 to get the square root curve. For example, an original grade of 90 will result in 94.87 after the square root curve is applied.
